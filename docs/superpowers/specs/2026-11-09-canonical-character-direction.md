# Canonical character appearance and animation

## Owner direction, 9 November 2026

All characters must be redrawn/rebuilt from the original game's source references, including proportions and animation. This applies to heroes, ordinary enemies and bosses. Attack animation includes anticipation, contact poses, enlarged/morphed limbs and recovery. Boss appearance is a complete source sequence, including hero reaction where present, rather than a generic fade or materialization.

The established project reference remains Battletoads USA NES at `feos-tas/DisAssemble@f1a43b69577246850d136473700a333b3cdfe93f`. The owner called it the 1989 game; no different source revision is inferred from that date. Preserve the existing 3D game while deriving silhouettes, proportions, palettes, poses, frame order and durations from verified original reference data. Do not substitute the 2020 redesign or an unrelated generated frog.

The clothed explorer candidate is a rejected study. Normal playable builds exclude its templates; its manifest and the generic cosmetic retarget adapter are retained for research only. The study does not count as a completed canonical Green, Yellow or Brown character.

## Work and evidence

1. Extract source-indexed character frames, palettes and metasprite bounds into a visual reference contact sheet. Record source revision and exact labels/indices.
2. Extract animation/state timelines, repeat/exit conditions and relevant gameplay locks. Label untraced behavior UNKNOWN instead of inventing it.
3. Rebuild models and animations from those references. Preserve the R6 gameplay body, actor identity, health, collision and server authority while replacing cosmetic presentation.
4. Integrate verified timelines, including first boss introduction, then verify their beginning, active phase, cancellation, death and group isolation in Studio.
5. Extend the same process to every remaining family and boss in the full-game catalog. A first-route improvement is not acceptance of all characters or all thirteen modes.

English remains the source language for visible text, with standard Roblox localization. Resources, life limits and human/NPC role rules remain unchanged by art direction.
