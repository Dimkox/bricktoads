# Green brawler — owner-directed 3D revision

`green-brawler.glb` is a new mesh revision based on the owner's detailed 9 November 2026 description, recorded in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-green-brawler-art-direction.md`. It supersedes the plain study for this art direction. It is an authored 3D adaptation; its depth, anatomy, sunglasses, cloth and metal details were not extracted from NES data. The earlier three `canonical-hero*.glb` files remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

The geometry includes a continuous sculpted V torso with flattened pectorals, sternum groove, segmented abdominals and obliques; massive shoulders, arms, clenched short-finger fists, muscular legs and broad toad feet; a small low-set head, closed asymmetric smirk, angular black sunglasses with physical frames and small highlights. Skin warts and creases, red cloth wraps above the elbows with hanging ends, and dark knee cuffs with silver rims, rivets and short conical spikes are actual geometry. No armour, gloves, weapon, hair, ears, tail, head horns, text or logo are included.

## Import and rig

- **Exactly 15 named cosmetic meshes**, one primitive per mesh and one shared glTF material. Accessories are merged into the relevant existing body segments; they do not add nodes, bones, a Humanoid or physics parts.
- `green-brawler-rig.json` contains every world-space pivot, parent, joint attachment, part bounds, bounding-box centre/size and neutral coordinate axes. Forward is **−Z**, up **+Y**, right **+X**. No shoulder rest correction is required.
- Authoring metadata uses **studs**. Both GLB vertex positions and hierarchical translations use **metres**, with **0.28 metres per stud**. Neutral model height is **6.074600065 studs / 1.700888018 metres**; the ground is exactly **Y = −3 studs**. Runtime physics/character height remains the caller's responsibility.
- The neutral rig is symmetric and animated at runtime. The asymmetric pose in `green-brawler-preview.png` is a review pose, not a baked or permanent game pose.
- **SurfaceAppearance correction:** the first Studio import showed that compiled `SurfaceAppearance` ignores mesh `COLOR_0`. The final `BrawlerBakedSurface` therefore stores all macro skin/accessory colour in its **2048×2048 baked albedo**, with no `COLOR_0` attribute. The three embedded PNG maps are baked albedo, ORM (occlusion in R, roughness in G, metalness in B), and an OpenGL normal map. Each triangle receives its own 8×8 UV cell with dilated padding. Material-role transitions are sampled within their own source cells; interpolation cannot cross into an unrelated material cell. Normal detail is transferred into the new UV tangent basis.
- The final GLB contains **no emissive texture, image or factor**. Physical sunglasses highlights remain geometry. The old four `green-brawler-*.png` maps are authoring/software-preview inputs; the final embedded maps have external copies named `green-brawler-baked-albedo.png`, `green-brawler-baked-orm.png` and `green-brawler-baked-normal.png`.
- The importer/clone adapter must preserve `SurfaceAppearance`, mesh UVs and neutral joint placements. Assigning additional materials or splitting accessory shells into separate MeshParts changes the 15-part contract. The completed texture pack must be checked in Studio; temporary importer vertex-colour previews are not acceptance evidence.

## Verification and previews

`green-brawler-validation.json` records 53,400 triangles in total, a maximum of 13,092 triangles in any mesh, closed orientable authoring-geometry components, valid nonzero faces, unit normals, UV ranges, and an independent decode of GLB world bounds against the 0.28 conversion. The surface bake duplicates triangle corners for unique UVs: 160,200 exported vertices in total, at most 39,276 in a mesh. Part records distinguish authoring vertices from exported vertices. `green-brawler-surface-bake.json` verifies the exact same ordered triangle positions/normals and identical node transforms before/after the conversion. Multiple overlapping closed components within a cosmetic segment are intentional for details and joint coverage; this is not a claim of a single watertight outer shell or a self-intersection-free solid.

`green-brawler-khronos.json` records the official Khronos validator result for the frozen SHA. Rebuild with `build_green_brawler.py` (it finishes with `bake_green_brawler_surface.py` automatically); validate with `node validate_green_brawler.cjs <path-to-gltf-validator-package>`. The bake refuses already-baked inputs; rebuild the authoring model first when regenerating.

`green-brawler-turntable.png` shows neutral front, three-quarter and side projections. `green-brawler-preview.png` shows the requested fighting pose, dark warm background and preview-only platform. Both are renders of the actual mesh, not generated concept images. The lightweight software renderer approximates lighting/material response; Roblox Studio is the acceptance renderer for PBR, silhouettes, accessory visibility and animation.

Still pending: actual Studio import and confirmation of 15 MeshParts/appearance maps, runtime bend/animation/network/device checks, source-derived oversized fist/boot attack morphs, and whole-body boss-appearance fear poses. This asset does not imply that all characters or the whole game are complete.
