# Physical Rat Race rival and bomb outcomes

`CampaignRace.lua` replaces the elapsed/deadline interpolation of a decorative rat with one real `RacingRat` actor, reserved actor ID `-98`. The source establishes a special course rival, not an ordinary three-heart opponent. Its selectable role therefore reports `resourceKind='CourseRival'` with no heart resource. It never uses an effect/projectile ID or creates an actor when claimed.

The pinned primary source is [feos-tas/DisAssemble, commit f1a43b69577246850d136473700a333b3cdfe93f, Battletoads/main.asm](https://github.com/feos-tas/DisAssemble/blob/f1a43b69577246850d136473700a333b3cdfe93f/Battletoads/main.asm). The local source inspected is `build/canonical-source/main.asm`, SHA-256 `71042cf2a4a8fad952e9eb3bcb591cece666c5a790d7ea177b1908d052fca074`. Jira context was read from the cached BT-52 level passport and BT-10 accepted decisions in `build/full-mechanics-jira-2026-11-09.json`.

All studs, seconds, body dimensions, velocities, impulse magnitudes, input mappings, the two-second initial claim window, the seeded stair arrangement, steering planner and checkpoint policy are original Roblox tuning. Rat Race source bytes, map/track coordinates, art, music and effects are not copied. The rat body is four studs tall around its movement root (three below and one above), radius1.3; this leaves real head clearance between the authored six-stud-spaced, two-stud-thick floors.

NPC and human owners use the same motor, speed, gravity, collision, supported hop and bomb contact. The NPC chooses which authored physical edge to leave and must fall onto the next support; route waypoints never place the actor. Validated human Move steers that body, Jump uses current support, and primary attack returns `NO_ACTION` because no racer attack was verified. Beginning movement or a hero targeting the racer permanently closes transfer. An incoming NPC hit does not close transfer on its own. Disconnect clears new steering and leaves finite reaction, falling and contact running; it never starts NPC control.

The same role/body identity persists across the original Roblox three-round course. A successful defuse authorizes the next authored start. A failed round remains failed until Session commits a new hero attempt; then only the current bomb round restarts. Earlier defuses stay resolved. The component neither writes hero health/reserves nor spawns heroes, reconnect heals, recreates the racer after its physical death, or starts a new run. Course-rival ownership remains assigned through a retry; death or completion spends the existing role life. This round policy is an adaptation, not a claim that the source's camera-based restart branches have been reproduced exactly.

`tests/run_campaign_race.luau` exercises the real Session/Core/Combat lifecycle through an in-memory world boundary. Its sixteen tests cover a real spawn and preserved identity on claim, blocked movement beyond an elapsed deadline, swept bomb contact, individual lifecycle deaths and reserve accounting, fresh valid/invalid defuses, finite nonlethal hit/stun, supported jump, offline steering and stale connection rejection, permanent targeted-attack transfer closure, accepted physical reactions during the initial claim window, packet replay and actor forgery rejection, committed-attempt retry, offline trio reserve conservation and old-level cleanup. All three rounds of four seeded authored stair layouts reach actual rat/bomb contact through physical movement. These are deterministic contracts, not Roblox engine collision, device controls, natural NES appearance counts, source timing parity, or complete presentation acceptance.
